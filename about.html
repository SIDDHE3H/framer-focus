Exploring modern website creation.

Framer Focus is a simple student blog created to explore Framer and the changing world of visual web design.

Purpose

This blog explains Framer in simple language and looks at how visual website tools can help students, designers and digital marketers create modern web experiences.

What you will learn

You will find an introduction to Framer, its features, uses, advantages and important considerations when choosing a website-building workflow.
